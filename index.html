FAQ

Co-parent calendar questions

What is Duet Co-parent Calendar?

Duet is an iPhone calendar for co-parents to plan custody days, parenting time, holidays, handoffs, events, chats, and shared schedule changes.

Can both parents share the same calendar?

Yes. Duet supports private iCloud sharing so both parents can keep one shared family calendar in sync.

Does Duet support pickup reminders?

Yes. Duet supports pickup reminders, Home Screen widgets, and Lock Screen Live Activity support for active handoffs.

Support and privacy

Questions about Duet?

Duet uses Apple's iCloud and CloudKit for family sharing. The app does not run its own account system for shared calendar data, and child photos stay local on each device. Widgets and pickup reminders use your Duet schedule to keep upcoming handoffs easier to see.
