A few helpful answers

Before you
make a plan.

What is Duet?

Duet is a shared co-parenting and family calendar for iPhone and iPad. Keep parenting days, handoffs, holidays, events, topics, tasks, and files together. Separated mode adds parenting-time tools; Married mode focuses on one-household coordination.

Is Duet free to use?

Duet is free to download, and private calendar use starts free. Optional subscriptions unlock sharing and additional features. An invited co-parent can join and use the shared calendar without a second subscription. Check the App Store and the in-app plan screen for current pricing.

How do both parents share a calendar?

Create your family calendar in Duet, then send the other parent an iCloud sharing invitation. They open the invitation on a supported Apple device and join your shared family. Both parents need iCloud to use family sharing.

Can Duet suggest a parenting schedule?

On supported devices, Smart Proposals use Private Cloud Compute to draft a 2-2-3, weekly, or custom parenting schedule. Parents review the suggested changes before they reach the shared calendar. AI features depend on device and feature availability.

Are shared expenses available now?

Yes. Duet 1.18 includes shared expense tracking with equal, percentage, or share-based splits, balances, recorded settlements, separate support tracking, and optional alerts. Duet records payments made outside the app; it does not move money.

Does Duet work on Android or in a web browser?

The Duet app is available for iPhone and iPad, requiring iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. There is no Android or browser version of the shared family app. The free custody schedule calculator on this website works in a web browser.

Can I use Duet without a connection?

You can open cached family information when offline. Sharing and receiving updates need a connection. Review sync status when reconnecting so both parents are working from the same plan.
