Custody handoff calendar

Give every transition a time and place

A custody day is clearer when the handoff details are attached: when, where, who transports, and what changes because of school, travel, or an event.

What belongs on a handoff calendar

Use one shared source for the repeating rotation and its exceptions. Add exact transition details to avoid different assumptions about when one parent's time ends and the other parent's begins.

For every handoff

  • Date, time, and location.
  • Which parent handles transportation.
  • School, daycare, or direct-home transition.
  • Related activity, appointment, or travel details.

For changed handoffs

  • The original and replacement dates.
  • Whether makeup time is included.
  • Who agreed to the change.
  • When the normal schedule resumes.
