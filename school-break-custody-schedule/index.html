School break custody schedule

Make the override and return explicit

School breaks often replace the usual weekly rotation. Record when the exception begins, who has each block, and exactly when the regular schedule resumes.

Plan each break as a dated exception

Use the school's actual closure calendar, not just the holiday name. A break can start after school, at a set evening time, or on the next morning, and those details affect both travel and overnight counts.

Before the break

  • Confirm the official school closure dates.
  • Define pickup time and location.
  • Add travel and activity commitments.
  • Identify which regular days are overridden.

After the break

  • Record the return handoff precisely.
  • State whether the normal rotation resumes or resets.
  • Add any makeup parenting time.
  • Share updates before travel begins.
