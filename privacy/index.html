Duet Co-parent Calendar Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Duet Co-parent Calendar is an iOS app for shared parenting schedules, holidays, events, topic chats, widgets, and pickup reminders. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information Duet Uses

  • Calendar setup details such as parent names, colors, custody days, holidays, events, and topic chats.
  • iCloud sharing information needed to create or accept a shared family calendar.
  • Optional child photos selected in the app.
  • Basic product analytics, such as app setup and feature usage events, to improve the app.
  • Support information you choose to send by email.

How Information Is Stored

Shared calendar data uses Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services. Duet does not run its own account system for your shared family calendar. Optional child photos stay local on each device and are not uploaded by Duet.

Subscriptions And Purchases

Duet uses StoreKit for in-app purchases and subscriptions. Purchase and subscription status is handled by Apple. Duet uses that status only to determine whether paid sharing features should be available.
