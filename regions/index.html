Useful planning tools, wherever your family lives

The website’s free schedule calculator works in a browser before the Duet app launches in your country. Compare rotations, count overnights and print a plan. Add local school closures and the holidays your family observes when reviewing the result.

Local dates and local currencies

The prepared app update adds regional holiday choices and expense entry in six currencies: USD, CAD, GBP, EUR, AUD and NZD. Dates remain reviewable, and balances stay separate by currency. State, province, regional and school calendars still need your local details.

Check availability before inviting the other parent

Duet’s shared family app requires supported iPhone or iPad devices and iCloud accounts. The app is currently distributed only in the United States. The regional pages explain upcoming availability and planned local subscription prices; they are not confirmation that either parent can download the app yet.
