Useful planning tools, wherever your family lives

The website’s free schedule calculator works in a browser without installing the app. Compare rotations, count overnights and print a plan. Add local school closures and the holidays your family observes when reviewing the result.

Local dates and local currencies

Duet 1.19 is available in the United States, Canada, the UK, Ireland, Australia and New Zealand. It adds regional holiday choices and expense entry in six currencies: USD, CAD, GBP, EUR, AUD and NZD. Dates remain reviewable, and balances stay separate by currency. State, province, regional and school calendars still need your local details.

Check availability before inviting the other parent

Duet’s shared family app requires supported iPhone or iPad devices and iCloud accounts. The app is available in the United States, Canada, the UK, Ireland, Australia and New Zealand. Check each parent’s storefront before inviting them. Each regional page shows local subscription prices and a direct App Store link.
