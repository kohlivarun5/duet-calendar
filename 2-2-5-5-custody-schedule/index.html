How the 2-2-5-5 rotation works

One parent typically has the same two weekdays, the other has the next two, and the parents alternate the five-day block that follows. Over two weeks, each parent receives seven overnights.

Pros

  • School-night routines stay consistent.
  • Weekends alternate evenly.
  • Each parent gets both short and long stretches.

Watchouts

  • Five-day gaps may be difficult for younger children.
  • Midweek transitions need clear handoff details.
  • Holidays can interrupt fixed-weekday expectations.
