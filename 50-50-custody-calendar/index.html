Common 50/50 custody calendar options

Families often use 2-2-3, 2-2-5-5, 3-4-4-3, or week-on/week-off schedules to split parenting time evenly. The best fit depends on commute, school age, handoff tolerance, and whether fixed weekdays matter.

Pros

  • Both parents have substantial weekday and weekend time.
  • Overnights usually balance across each two-week cycle.
  • Several templates can match different work schedules.

Watchouts

  • Equal time still needs clear rules for holidays and school breaks.
  • Some rotations create frequent transitions.
  • Monthly percentages may vary slightly because months have different lengths.
