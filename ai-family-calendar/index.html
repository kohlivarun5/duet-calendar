What does an AI family calendar do?

An AI family calendar can help turn unstructured information into a useful draft. In Duet, that means suggested events and tasks from things you share, or a proposed parenting-day schedule. Parents decide what to keep, edit, or leave out before anything is added.

Turn a school email into next steps

  1. Share the source with Duet. Send an email, photo, file, or text from another app using the share sheet.
  2. Review the suggestions. On supported devices, AI identifies possible events and concrete tasks.
  3. Check the important details. Compare dates, times, locations, and assignments with the original information.
  4. Edit and add. Choose the items you want in your family’s plan and keep the source with the relevant context.

One camp email, several useful reminders

A camp email might include orientation, a permission form, and a packing list. Review the suggested orientation event, check the sign-up task, and keep the original details nearby in topics and files.

Draft a parenting schedule with Smart Proposals

In Separated mode, Smart Proposals use Private Cloud Compute on supported devices to draft a 2-2-3, weekly, or custom parenting schedule. You can review the proposed days before the shared calendar changes. Holiday assignments stay locked, and existing events can help suggest clearer handoff timing.

If you are still choosing a rotation, start with the free custody schedule calculator. Compare the shape of different months, then recreate or draft your chosen rotation in Duet. The website preview does not automatically import into the app.

Parents make the final call

AI can misread a date or miss context. Check every suggestion against the original note and your family’s commitments. Share a proposal with your co-parent, compare the calendar, and discuss changes before approval. For individual date exchanges, use Swap Requests.

Which devices support AI features?

AI functionality depends on the feature, device, and system availability. Duet’s event and task suggestions use on-device AI on supported devices; Smart Proposals use Private Cloud Compute on supported devices. Installing Duet does not by itself guarantee that every AI feature is available.

The core calendar, family events, topics, and tasks remain useful without AI. See the App Store listing for current app compatibility and the privacy policy for data-handling information.

Questions about ai family calendar & smart parenting plans

Does AI add events without my review?

No. Duet presents suggested events and tasks for you to review and edit before adding them.

Does Smart Proposal drafting change assigned holidays?

Holiday assignments stay locked during Smart Proposal drafting. Review all suggested parenting-day changes before approval.

Can I use the calendar without AI?

Yes. Duet’s calendar, shared events, topics, and tasks do not require you to use AI assistance. AI features have additional device and availability requirements.
