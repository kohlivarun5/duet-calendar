How alternating weeks work

Parents alternate seven-night blocks, often changing homes after school or at a set weekend time. The simple rhythm can reduce handoffs and make activities easier to plan.

Pros

  • Only one regular transition each week.
  • Both homes handle full school and weekend routines.
  • Easy to remember and project ahead.

Watchouts

  • A full week apart can be difficult for younger children.
  • School items and medications need reliable handoff routines.
  • Holiday and vacation overrides can shift the weekly rhythm.
