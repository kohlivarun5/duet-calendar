How alternating weekends usually work

In a basic alternating weekends schedule, one parent has most school nights while the other parent has every other weekend. Some plans add a midweek dinner or overnight; this calculator shows the simple every-other-weekend base pattern.

Pros

  • Easy to understand and repeat.
  • Lower handoff frequency than many equal-time schedules.
  • Often fits school-week routines and longer-distance co-parenting.

Watchouts

  • Parenting-time percentages are not 50/50 in the simple version.
  • Long gaps can happen without midweek time or calls.
  • Holiday weekends need explicit exception rules.
