How the 3-4-4-3 rotation works

One parent has three nights, the other has four, then the sequence reverses. The pattern reduces transitions compared with 2-2-3 while keeping an equal two-week split.

Pros

  • Balanced time in a two-week cycle.
  • Fewer transitions than 2-2-3.
  • Predictable three- and four-night blocks.

Watchouts

  • Weekday responsibility can shift between weeks.
  • Four-night gaps may not fit every child.
  • Holiday exceptions need a shared source of truth.
