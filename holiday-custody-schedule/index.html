How to plan holiday custody schedules

Most families use a regular weekly or two-week rotation, then override it for major holidays, school breaks, birthdays, and travel. A clear calendar helps both parents see when the exception starts, when it ends, and how the normal rhythm resumes.

Pros

  • Separate holiday rules prevent confusion around the base schedule.
  • Alternating-year holidays can be planned ahead.
  • Shared visibility reduces last-minute texting before breaks.

Watchouts

  • Holidays may override regular weekends or weekdays.
  • Pickup and return times should be written down clearly.
  • Travel, school closure days, and makeup time may need separate entries.
